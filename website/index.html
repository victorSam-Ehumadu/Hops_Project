What you get

Built for the moments signal drops out

Every core feature runs peer-to-peer over Bluetooth — no cell service, no campus Wi-Fi, and no central server reading your messages.

🚨

Emergency Flare

Alerts everyone with Hops open nearby, and relays through their phones to reach further than your Bluetooth alone. Triggered from your phone’s quick-settings tile, so you don’t have to unlock and open the app first.

📡

Radar & Contacts

See who's nearby, send friend requests, and chat directly — device to device, never through a server.

👀

Watch Me

Let a friend keep an eye on your distance from them in real time — handy for a late walk home.

📣

Community Alerts

Official broadcasts from campus admins reach every nearby Hopper instantly, verified and signed.

🎮

Hops Hopper & Wordle

Two built-in mini-games — earn carrots as you play, and spend them on exclusive looks in the Hops Shop.

🔒

Private by design

Your phone broadcasts a code that changes every 15 minutes instead of your name, so a passing scanner can’t follow you around campus. Messages, tasks and alerts stay on your phone — there’s no server holding them.

How it works

Three steps, one of them online

Hops needs a connection once, to create your account. After that the mesh runs on Bluetooth alone.

Install and sign up

Download the app and create an account. This is the only step that needs the internet — and the last time you'll need it.

Your phone says hello

Hops broadcasts a short code over Bluetooth that changes every 15 minutes, and listens for the same from phones around you. No pairing, no accounts between you.

Messages hop onward

Every phone that hears an alert passes it on, so a call for help travels further than any single phone can reach.

Ready when you are

Get Hops on your phone

Free to download. Sign-up takes a moment and needs a connection once; after that, everything that matters works with no signal at all.
