Your campus, always in reach.

Hops is an offline-first safety and social app for campus — it works entirely over Bluetooth, so it keeps working exactly when Wi-Fi and signal don't.

Download Hops How it works

Built for the moments signal drops out

Every core feature runs peer-to-peer over Bluetooth — no cell service, no campus Wi-Fi, and no central server reading your messages.

🚨

Emergency Flare

One tap alerts everyone with Hops open nearby — and relays through their phones to reach further than your Bluetooth alone.

📡

Radar & Contacts

See who's nearby, send friend requests, and chat directly — device to device, never through a server.

👀

Watch Me

Let a friend keep an eye on your distance from them in real time — handy for a late walk home.

📣

Community Alerts

Official broadcasts from campus admins reach every nearby Hopper instantly, verified and signed.

🎮

Hops Hopper & Wordle

Two built-in mini-games — earn carrots as you play, and spend them on exclusive looks in the Hops Shop.

🔒

Private by design

Your Bluetooth name rotates every 15 minutes, and chats are end-to-end — nothing to track, nothing to leak.

Ready when you are

Free to download, works the moment you open it — no account required to try Radar and Emergency Flare.
